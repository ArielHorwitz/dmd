Suggest 20 project names formed by concatenating the names of two animals into one lowercase word.
Requirements:
- The name should roll off the tongue and be easy to say, spell, and remember.
- Prefer combinations with a natural rhythm, clean consonant transitions, and no awkward vowel collisions.
- Each animal should loosely symbolize some aspect of the project. For example:
  - Birds can suggest oversight, coordination, visibility, or high-level operations.
  - Fast animals can suggest speed and responsiveness.
  - Defensive or resilient animals can suggest security and reliability.
  - Social animals can suggest cooperation or distributed systems.
  - Clever or dexterous animals can suggest automation and control.
- The symbolism does not need to be rigorous; it should simply provide a satisfying story for the name.
- Avoid forced portmanteaus: use recognizable animal names joined directly together.
- Avoid names that sound cumbersome, overly long, aggressive, or like random word salad.
- Give a one-line rationale for each suggestion.
- Finish by identifying the five strongest candidates and explain which one best fits the project.
- A useful selection test is: “Can I say it once in conversation and expect someone to remember and spell it correctly?”
